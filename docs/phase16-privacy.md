## Privacy notice draft

Court Assist is a private practice workspace for advocates. The service uses your account and profile information to operate the workspace. Records you add can contain case and party details, client contacts, scanned legal documents, financial entries, research, reminders and drafts. Enter records you are authorized to use and protect client confidentiality. Your workspace is scoped to your account. Providers used by the deployed service may include the operator's infrastructure, storage, delivery, crash reporting, and optional AI providers. Before launch, list the provider names, purposes, regions, retention and subprocessor policies, and contact details.

Document storage uses private access controls and short-lived download links. Offline storage is optional. Cached content and selected vault copies use device encryption as described in Phase 15. The app uses generic notification previews by default. Local copies, exported files, and files sent to an external viewer may be governed by the device and viewer. The operator processes limited security and operational events to detect abuse, support availability and investigate incidents.

AI is optional. It is off by default and uses only data selected for the specific request. The app records provider-specific approval and separate consent for document processing and external transmission where relevant. Provider access and deletion follow the documented provider terms. Changes to the provider or consent can require reconfirmation. Review every generated result before using it. See [AI processing and consent](phase14-ai.md).

Contact **[privacy and grievance contact]** to ask about access, correction, export, deletion, or consent. Include your request reference where available. Publish the operator name, postal address, response process and secure communication channel here before release.

## Terms draft

Court Assist is licensed to individual authorized advocates for managing their own practice. Protect your account credentials and devices. Keep the information you enter accurate and upload materials you have the right to use. Verify templates, legal references, AI suggestions, dates and calculations before relying on them. The service prepares records and documents for your review. You remain responsible for filings, deadlines, legal advice, professional obligations and retention of your practice records. Complete operator-specific terms for service availability, dispute resolution, intellectual property, changes, termination and applicable consumer rights with counsel.

## Export, deletion, and retention

- Provide workspace export through an identity-verified support request. Verify the authenticated advocate without asking for their password. Offer existing private vault downloads and supported finance and drafting exports. For a full copy, prepare a controlled owner-scoped export under operator review, exclude authentication secrets and provider tokens, encrypt the package, and deliver through an expiring authenticated channel. Record the request, completion and deletion of the delivery copy. A self-service full-workspace export has not been implemented.
- **Settings → Security → Request account deletion** asks for the current password, disables server access, revokes sessions and push registrations, and presents a receipt. It clears the requesting device's local content. Other disconnected devices may retain encrypted work through the existing offline session window. Provide the external account deletion resource and a secure support route for users who cannot sign in.
- Verify the deletion request, export need and any valid preservation requirements. Disable processing for that owner, wait for already issued URLs and in-flight operations to expire, verify storage prefixes and backups, and run the operator purge from a container with the profile volume and erasure-specific credentials. The command is `python -m app.services.account_erasure <receipt-uuid> --confirm-erasure`. It is retryable and removes current S3 objects and versions, profile images and owner database records. It retains a completion receipt with the account identifier cleared. For a storage or constraint failure, investigate and retry before notifying the user. Check copies held by subprocessors and backups. If restoring a pre-erasure backup, apply the restricted erasure ledger before allowing traffic. Keep that ledger access-limited and retained only for the approved recovery window.
- Proposed defaults for review are account data for the duration of the account, short lifecycle expiry for unconfirmed uploads, backup expiry following the recovery schedule, and AI content expiry according to the disclosed settings. Set operational log retention according to verified obligations. Record any legal hold, scope, decision owner, review date and release action. Keep legal or financial records and access events only for the applicable purpose and period. The operator must configure and verify actual retention.

## Incident response

Assign an incident lead, a privacy contact, a security contact and an alternate. Triage whether private legal information, credentials or signing keys were exposed. Contain by isolating affected systems, stopping unnecessary processing, revoking exposed sessions, rotating relevant credentials, and narrowing access. Preserve relevant timestamps, request IDs and evidence in restricted encrypted storage. Assess notification duties and clocks immediately with counsel. Notify affected parties and regulators as required, keeping notices factual. Restore in isolation, verify data integrity, document the timeline and decisions, and review the controls after recovery.
